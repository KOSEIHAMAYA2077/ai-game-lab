# WordNet-derived vocabulary

The bundled `konjo-wordnet.json` is a small modified selection of noun-synset
lemmas. Its shape associations, sense selection and exclusions were authored for
this prototype; they are not endorsed by the dictionary providers.

broad or misleading for this artwork; NFC normalization; English underscores
replaced by spaces and English letters lowercased. No model was trained and no
relation outside the selected synsets is represented as a WordNet synonym.
